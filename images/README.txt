PHOTOS GO IN THIS FOLDER

(The decor/ folder holds the site's design graphics: the honeycomb texture and the gear.
 Don't delete it.)

Suggested subfolders (create them when you need them):
  images/team/       team member photos (square, e.g. 400x400 pixels)
  images/buildlog/   build log photos
  images/robot/      robot photos and CAD renders

Before adding a photo:
  1. Resize it. About 1600 pixels wide is plenty (team photos: 400x400).
     Try to keep each file under 500 KB so the site stays fast on phones.
     Free tool: https://squoosh.app (works in your browser).
  2. Name it in lowercase with hyphens and no spaces, e.g. intake-prototype-v2.jpg
     (GitHub Pages is case-sensitive: Photo.JPG and photo.jpg are different files.)
  3. When you add it to a page, always write alt text describing what it shows.

Do not add FIRST or FTC logos until the team has checked FIRST's brand guidelines.
