Welcome

The Bees Knees is a FIRST Tech Challenge team from the Baccalaureate School for Global Education in Astoria, Queens. After six years away from competition, we are back for the 2026–27 season: designing, building, and programming a competitive robot while laying the foundation for a program that lasts.
