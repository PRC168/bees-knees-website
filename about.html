Who We Are

A revived FIRST Tech Challenge team building a lasting engineering program at BSGE.

01 / Our story

The Bees Knees (FTC Team #13645) is a FIRST Tech Challenge team at the Baccalaureate School for Global Education (BSGE) in Astoria, Queens, New York. The 2026–27 season marks our return to competition for the first time in six years. That means we are rebuilding the team from the ground up: recruiting members, setting up our workflows, and designing our robot together from first principles.

We are not starting from nothing. In recent years, Stingbotics, our fellow BSGE FTC team, has been the school's primary FTC team, and we are proud to work alongside them. Two teams at one school means more students getting hands-on engineering experience, and a stronger robotics community at BSGE.

Our focus this season is the engineering process: defining problems clearly, prototyping quickly, testing honestly, and iterating based on what we learn. We document our work in our build log so that every lesson is passed on, because our goal is not just one good season, but a program that keeps growing long after this year's team graduates.

02 / Mission & values

Our mission is to build a sustainable robotics program at BSGE where students learn real engineering by doing it: designing, building, programming, and documenting a competitive robot as a team.

Value 01

Engineering process

We make decisions based on testing and evidence, not guesswork. Every design goes through prototyping, testing, and iteration.

Value 02

Learning first

Every member should leave the season with new skills, whether in CAD, programming, fabrication, or communication.

Value 03

Documentation

We record what we try and why, so future members can build on our work instead of starting over.

Value 04

Gracious Professionalism

We compete hard and respectfully, help other teams when we can, and treat everyone we work with as a colleague.
